Active & Passive Voice

Prototype study note · Created for private review


The central pattern
For a straightforward active clause with a direct object, the object can become the subject of the passive clause. Use the appropriate form of be + past participle (V3).
Active: Ravi writes a letter.
Passive: A letter is written by Ravi.

Keep the tense

• Simple present: writes → is / am / are written
• Simple past: wrote → was / were written
• Present continuous: is writing → is being written
• Present perfect: has written → has been written
• Simple future: will write → will be written
• Modal: can write → can be written

Watch the pronouns
She helps him.
He is helped by her.

Common errors and limits
V3 is mandatory in the standard passive construction. “A letter is wrote” is incorrect; use “written”. The form of “be” must also agree with the new subject.
Intransitive verbs such as “arrive” have no direct object and do not form an ordinary object-based passive. “He arrived” has no standard passive transformation.
The “by” phrase is optional when the agent is unknown, unimportant or obvious: “The window was broken.”


Exam trap
Do not change the tense while transforming the sentence. “Ravi wrote a letter” becomes “A letter was written by Ravi”, not “is written”.
